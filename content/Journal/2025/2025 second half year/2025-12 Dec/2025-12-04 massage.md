Started the morning with taking the bus to Robertsfors, which meant a transfer at Sikeå, as the 12 that leaves  Lövånger at 07:00 doesn't leave the highway to go into Robertsfors. This was the first time I had to do a transfer, so I was paranoid about missing it. It is only 30 minutes between Lövånger and Sikeå by bus, so not worth getting the computer out, so I just played with my phone instead.

There was about a three minutes wait in Sikeå before the next bus arrived, so not that long, and then nine minutes more to get all the way into the town. I wouldn't want to make a habit of this trip, not enough time on the bus to accomplish anything, but still a decent chunk out of the day.

But, back on the day I booked it, getting a massage sounded worth the hassle of needing multiple buses. In practice, yes, the massage was worth it. He asked what I needed, and I replied that it has been ages since my last massage, so everything, but especially shoulders. 

Hopefully, they will open a Lövånger branch soon, and it won't be necessary to take the bus.
