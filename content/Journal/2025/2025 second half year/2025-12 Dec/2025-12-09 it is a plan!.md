Of course, it will take time (they won't start till mid January), but still, I am very excited!

I tried to follow, but it wasn't easy, and there was nothing I could do to contribute.

Therefore, I wasn't surprised to need a long lunch, with a book, afterwards. Now that I have a working one of those, any chances I make in the Obsidian vault get pushed to GitHub, and then on to the web page, and I am pleased. (which is why I have provided the above link.)

Hopefully, this means I can also get this blog working.
