Since today is *valborgsmässoafton* and we are joining the other folk in the Shire at one of the City's big bonfire celebrations to cook and sell hamburgers and fika to the public, but it is also a half-day for work, I opted to drive in to town with Keldor this morning, to work at his dad's while he works, and then head to the event.

I only worked till 13:00, and then I went to Emma's "Been in the new house two years, time for a house-warming party", where I made good progress adding beads to the antlers of the reindeer on [[My viking coat]]

Keldor came by after he was done working, and then we went to town for Valborg I was one of the ones wirking the grill. We used the grill the church provided. I cannot recommend that grill, which has bars widely enough spaced that multiple "pariser" (thick slices of sausage that are grilled and served on a hamburger bun) and hotdogs fell through and into the coals over the course of the evening.
