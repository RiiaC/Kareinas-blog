I finished the outline (started yesterday) of a reindeer head with knotwork antlers on the chest of [[My viking coat]] while Keldor armoured up.

They ran the tournament in a "Canterbury" style, which means each fighter held the field in turn, facing all of the other fighters, and each bout was fought best two of three. They had planned to run it as a Double Canterbury, meaning that after everyone had fought everyone else, they would start over and do it all again. I don't think my champion was alone in being grateful to hear that the range of points among the fighters was so widely distributed that their Majesties decided that a secont Canterbury round wouldn't be likely to change the outcome, so they jumped straight to a final round between Count Padrig and Sir Jan, which went all five rounds.
