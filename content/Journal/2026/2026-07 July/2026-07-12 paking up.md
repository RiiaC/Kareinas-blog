I woke, full of energy at 07:00 and started slowly packing up and taking things to the car as they were ready. After the others got up and had breakfast the packing and carrying went faster, and our part of camp was done early enough to help others with carrying things over the bridge.

Just before noon I was sent, along with Pär, who had charge of the kitchen this weekend, to get pizza for everyone who had stayed to help pack all of Reengarda's things. 

The plan was to use Reengarda's debit card, but we aren't certain what code is attached to it, so we asked to do it with signature instead, but the shop owner couldn't figure out how, so I just psid myself  and will transfer money to me later. 

By the time we returned, with 15 normal pizzas, 3 vegetarian,  1 fussy eater vegetarian,  and one gluten free, the others had pretty much gotten everything off the island and the island cleaned up. 

After eating I took Holmfrid and Charlotte back to the hiuse  while Keldor and Erin returned to the workshop to keep working on her helm.

We reached the house just after 14:00, and by 15:26 we had emptied the car, put the food into the fridge, spread the damp rugs and mattresses in the sun to dry.

I had expected to stop there (and the others were sensible enough to do so), but I kept working till most stuff had even been put away, for which my future self will be grateful. 

Then I got the "voluntary bridge tax" cash donations re-counted and into the cash box, and updated GnuCash with that, and the Swish deposits before doing my yoga and heading to bed
