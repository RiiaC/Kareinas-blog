Another very sunny morning. Had to open the tent front and back doors when I woke to let out the heat generated by five sleeping people.

The day was hot, sunny, and humid and low pressure, which gave  me less energy for rock carving than yesterday and the last couple of hours we were open,  I didn't even try, working on nålbindning instead.

After we closed for the day I continued to sit at the table, talking with people, and nålbindning, feeling  vaguely guilty that I wasn't helping people I could see working, taking down tents no longer needed, but I didn't have the energy. 

After a while, I  packed up the axes and knives from our table, and put away my rock carving stuff.

After a bit more relaxing and taking, I forced myself to get up and go get the car and move it to the parking by the bridge, after which I took the stone carving stuff and the for sale box over (three trips needed for thst, with the moving trolley).

During the first trip I called Keldor, who had taken Erin to the workshop at work to make more progress on the helmet, to ask what they thought about taking down camp tonight, when it is slightly cooler, and the sun isn't shining directly on us  they liked the idea, but when I asked Holmfrid, and Charlotte what they thought, Holmfrid was not wanting the event to end, while Charlotte was happy to do whatever works for us 

But by the time I did those three trips, I had no energy left to do more, so I sat on the ground and talked with Lucie and did more nålbindning, till Keldor and Erin returned to sute, and pointed out that they were singing over there (which I hadn't heard, but Lucie had been aware of for some time ). 

So I took my project over there  and enjoyed a handful of songs, till I tried ti start the second leg of my [[Nålbinded Dragon]], and realised I didn't understand what I just done to start the first one, and had no brain left at all.

At which poinr ai decided that I may as well go to sleep, so I did yoga, and was in bed before 23:00.

Then, only 40 minutes later, Keldor, Charlotte,  and Erin all came in and got ready for bed  which woke me just enough that I couldn't fall back asleep,  so I typed this instead.
