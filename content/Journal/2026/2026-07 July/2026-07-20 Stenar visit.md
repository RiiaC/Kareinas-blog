I spent my work day playing with my SEAD structure diagram using Obsidian's Canvas tool, having had a brainstorm on a way to approach it. 

Rather than doing a classic database diagram, with each table appearing once, and connected to everything else it is connected to, I am opting to present groups, each group with one central table, and surrounded by all parent and child tables that connect to the one in the center. Then I show connections between groups, rather than pointing to exactly the right table in a group, but use colour coding to show which tables appear in more than one group. 

It is a fun approach to work on, but it took all day to figure out "rules" to decide how the colours work for those tables that appear in lots of groups.
