This weekend Sweden has an election. Unlike certain countries best not named, we have a many party system. Therefore, the SVT, the Swedish TV station, offers a web page where you can take a quiz on the curent political question, and based on your answers it will match you percentage wise to the parties. Then, one can go in to the web pages of the ones for which you get the best matches, to see if they have any deal makers or breakers.
