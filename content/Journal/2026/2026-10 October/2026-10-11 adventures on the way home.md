# Ferry trip
We made it to the Ferry Terminal in Vaasa just after 18:00, which is perfect timing, as 18:00 is when they open the gate to the waiting area.

We hadn't bothered to pay for a cabin for the return ferry, so we just hung out in the bar and chatted as I made progress on fitting for my [[New winter coat]]. I managed to decide where, how to trim the body rectangles at the shoulder so that the arms will attach comfortably. 

However, as I was feeling tired, I opted not to cut that seam, yet, but just basted over the final chalk line to show where I should cut later.

We arrived at the terminal in Umeå at 23:00, disembarked and started driving at 23:16,  made it home at 00:36. We were too tired to unload more than the ice chest,  which we emptied into the fridge, and our pillows  which we needed.
