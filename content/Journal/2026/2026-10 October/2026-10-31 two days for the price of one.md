# Saturday, part two
When I woke it was nearly 16:00, and I thought "I should get up and head to the store before it closes". But then I saw an email from a friend with their flight info for coming to my Birthday Party/our New Years Party, so I took the time to answer that message. By then it was pretty much 16:30, and the local store closes at 17:00 on Saturdays, so we hopped up, got dressed, and went to the store. I wanted fruit and veg, and they had a reasonable variety. One of the things they had was spaghetti squash, so I picked one up, thinking it might be nice to cook up later this week. Then I accidentally dropped it on the floor (since my hands were also full of a bag of pears, and the combined load was awkward), and it cracked. Ok then, I can take a hint from the food gods. Buy this squash, and cook it as soon as I get home.

So I did, making a [[Steamed Veg and sauce]]. I steamed the spaghetti squash, cut into thirds and seeds removed, over water with a bit of mushroom powder added. While it was cooking I chopped up som zucchini, broccoli, and carrot, and set them aside, ready to steam when the squash was done. Then, as the squash wasn't done yet, I made a [[Red beet salad]] and ate some of that. I also ate the pumpkin seeds, which I had toasted in the toaster oven as everyting else cooked. By then the squash was soft, so I set it aside and started steaming the broccoli stems and carrot. While they cooked, I mixed the squash with some butter and spices to get it broken up into its component "spaghetti strands", and then added the cooked broccoli stems and carrot to the bowl as I steamed the zucchini and broccoli flowers. When they were cooked, I added them to the bowl and put some spinach directly into the steaming water, covered it, and let it get soft. Then I used an immersion blender to make a nice green sauce, to which I added ground peanuts and almonds, some tahini, some ginger, lots of spices, a bit of soy sauce, some nutritional yeast, and more butter. I stirred that into the veg, and added some sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, and flax seeds. This whole process, including cleaning the kitchen afterwards took more than 1.5 hours, but the result was really yummy, and there is plenty to eat over the course of the week.

After cooking I went to the computer and met Þórólfr in zoom to figure out how to use the vault sync for google drive plugin with the Reengarda Inventory vault. We got it working on my computer and phone, and removed that vault from my Obsidian Sync plan, but we didn't manage get him logged into that vault sync, so he will work on it later. Once he understands how, we can set it up for anyone else in the shire who wants to be able to edit the inventory from Obsidian.

While I had the vault open, I went through and fixed up what had been previously started--taking the new photos I had taken this summer, attaching them to their right notes, cropping those that needed it, renaming the older photos to also show their date taken as part of the name, and updating the README file. I stayed up way too late doing all this. Good thing I had done yoga this morning.
