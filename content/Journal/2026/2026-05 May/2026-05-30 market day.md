Having stayed up till after 02:00, I slept nearly six hours before feeling awake enough to contemplate facing the day. Since there was partial cloud cover, and I had opened the tent windows when I ran to the loo sometime after sunrise, and before properly waking, the temperature in the tent was still tolerable to do my yoga in the tent before breakfast.

Then I had time to wander around and chat with people before Meadhbh's vigil, which took place in the ground floor of the castle. I love daytime vigils, I am able to eat some of the snacks provided,  which, since they included fresh blueberries,  I was very happt to do.

We three "*Frostimetsä*" laurels (norra Nordmark/Frostheim and Aarnimetsä), Johanna,  Keldor,  and I, went in together, as I was the only one of us who had met Meadhbh before.

When we came in,  Meadhbh reminded me of [the time Kaarina and I visited Ireland for an event in 2018](https://kareina.dreamwidth.org/708947.html), when Kaarina asked Meadhbh why there was no Irish food on the menu, and Meadhbh replied "come back again, there will be". She certainly succeeded with that!
