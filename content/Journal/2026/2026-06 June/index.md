This is the blog part of my blog, where I keep the actual journal posts (written on the day, or within a short time of the adventures described), sorted into folders by years and parts of years, as needed. While I first started keeping an [online journal in 2005](https://kareina.dreamwidth.org/archive), most of those posts have not been migrated here yet, so I only have folders here for the most recent years. 

Note that there is also a folder for [Places I Have Lived](obsidian://open?vault=Kareinas-blog&file=content%2FJournal%2FPlaces%20I%20have%20lived%2Findex), which is a summary of where I have lived over the years, with highlight memories of each location, written after creating this version of my blog in 2025/2026 That section is a work in progress, containing only the first few years of my life so far.
