# suddenly productive 
So I sat at the computer, saw the file my colleague gave me, and started making a report of the terms that appear in the "species" column of the data set, which ones are for plants and animals already in the database, and which are new, and what terms are neither plant nor animal, and we might consider a different column for that info.
