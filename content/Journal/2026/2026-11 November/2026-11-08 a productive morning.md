# morning accomplishments 
Woke early enough, and with enough energy, to do a 30 minute yoga with HIT DownDog session, and do the final cleaning of yesterday's trattkantareller and get them into the food dehydrator before breakfast (7 trays!). 

Then I ate breakfast, brushed my teeth, noticed that it is time to replace my toothbrush, checked the cupboard, yes, there are two more bamboo toothbrushes in the box,  got one out and put it in the loo, then took the worn-out one downstairs, cut off the brush end, turned on Keldor’s [[Emco Star combination woodworking shop multitool|EmcoStar]], used the sanding disk to rough shape a nålbindning needle, and then added a hole hand sanded it,  polished it, and just like that, less than an hour from impulse to start a new project, my new bamboo nålbindning needle was done.

# getting ready
When I came back upstairs, Keldor was awake, so I showed off my new needle and bounced happily about my already productive morning. 

Then I ate second breakfast as he ate his first, and we played Qwirkle (he won, by a lot. After the game I packed food for the day and took it to my car, then went to transfer yesterday's projects from his car to mine.

When I opened the back of the van, I saw that the piece of mylonite that Keldor had brought home on Friday, along with the second hand [[wooden school lockers]] he had gotten from a colleague, was no longer only bent into the space between the lockers and the wall of the van, but had cracked.

So I pushed the lockers out of the way and carried it in to ask him iif he still wanted it. He was feeling stressed just then, not able to find some of the tools he wanted to take to *Hantverksträff*, and didn't react well to the combination the intruption and the news it had broken. I wasn't feeling particularly happy about more trash being brought home, and just wanted to know if we should put it asside to get rid of, or put it in the corner of the shop with other potentially useful large stuff, so I could get it out of the way of putting things in the car.

After a few exchanges in which neither of sounded happy, he got the information that it was already broken when I opened the door this morning, I hadn't broken it trying to get it out, and I just wanted to know if he still wants to keep it. He did, so I put it away in the shop, went upstairs, found out that he was stressed trying to find his leather sewing needle, helped him look, and after he found it and he got his project packed, he moved the car around to the garage, we rearranged a few things, and unloaded the [[wooden school lockers]].

This was my first good look at them, and I was feeling a bit skeptical about them. Then, as we discussed his vision of sanding and repainting them, we both realised that they would be good for holding power tools, and, in fact, if we put them in the cellar hallway where we currently have an open shelf holding an ugly mishmash of plastic boxes full of power tools, it wouldn't take any more space, but the collection would look better, and the tools easier to access. Suddenly, I was onboard with the acquisition!

Then we finished transering my projects to my car, and we were off to town.
# Hantverksträff
Despite the extra steps in getting ready needed to unload those lockers, we were still out the door early enough that we would have arrived at Hantverksträff before Petronilla, who has the key, so we picked her up on the way. 

I decided that it was time to look at the shoulders of the coat, and consulted with Petronilla, who could see the back. We decided to add a triangle gore to the back center of my upper back, to shift the seam for the sleeve attachment a bit further out, and then take off a corresponding amount from the front of the armscythe. I made decent progress on inserting that goal under the time we were there. 

Then we went over to Elgianten, to look at phones, as mine has had issues, especially with the battery for quite a while now. Alas, the phone I liked best is the[ Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8](https://www.samsung.com/se/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8), which is shaped and proportioned more like a book than a phone, and is so very appealing for that reason, but I really don't want to pay more than 20,000 kr for a phone. I didn't buy anything, but the salesman looked into options, and says that if I were willing to switch from Telnor to Telia for my phone service I could keep the same phone number, and save 2,000 kr on the asking price (but that is still more than 20,000 kr). Given that the non folding phones could be had for more like 5,000 to 7,000 kr, I suspect that my desire for the book phone won't lead anywhere.

Then it was home and relax for a bit before time for yoga and bed.
