Woke thinking of [[Create an attic bedroom#on 2026-11-16|the bed nook]] and how I can still have the nice big clothes hanging rack mounted on the center of the room, so I did a quick sketch. Then I got up, worked 8.5 hours. 

Keldor slept in (a smart thing to do when home from work for a sick day), and sometime after he got up he did some adjustments to the fit of the wrought iron door to make it not pop open on its own the second it is unlatched. Then he disappeared for a while

I finished hemming the curtains for the guestroom beds, cleaned that room, made the beds, cleaned the downstairs loo, and then tackled the pile that has been growing at the bottom of the stairs, and managed to beat it into a less disgusting shape. Some of that stuff I have no idea what it even is, if it is important, or trash, or what. But at least it is no longer in the way of getting to the guest room.
