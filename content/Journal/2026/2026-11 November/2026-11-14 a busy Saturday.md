We didn't get to bed till about 02:30 last night, I woke about 5:30 with hips feeling like they wanted me to get up and move. I tried just going to the loo, and returning to bed,  but no, they were insistent  and finally dragged me out of bed and to the living room for a short pilates session. Then I could finally return to bed. I slept till nearly 09:00, and then got up and had a very productive day.

We started with Qwirkle over breakfast (he won), then shovelled the slush/mud that the city had plowed over the entrance to the driveway. I am so disappointed in the nearly 2 cm of snow we got last night. While it was raining here, it was snowing in Skellefteå, to the north of us, where they got as much as 80 cm, and in Umeå to the south of us, where they got at least 15 or 20 cm. Why not here?

Ok, I confess, it was good that it did rain yesterday, when he was home, and he noticed the leak, so we could fix it (the rag we tied around the ventilation pipe was dry this morning). If it were a proper winter, we would have had snow that didn't melt, so leaking wouldn't have started till spring, by which point we would have finished the closet, and that pipe would have been enclosed in a dead space, and we wouldn't have seen the leak till it had done some serious damage.

The next project of the day was finally fixing the coat rack by the front door, which has been pulling away from the wall and threatening to fall for a while now. So we emptied it, re-attached it to the wall quite a bit higher than it had been, so now there is a gap between the coats and the shoe rack, and I think it looks much better.

![[2026-11-14 coat rack repaired.jpg|500]]

Then Keldor took a nap and I finished cleaning up after that project. I considered sleeping, too, but decided that as long as we were in "get a round to it" mode, I should do the work needed to attach the wrought iron door inside the front door, so we can get rid of the annoying inner door that opens the wrong direction.

![[2026-11-14 starting carving.jpg|500]]

Step one was to chisel away a 1 cm deep bit of the threshold, as the door is a bit too tall for this door-frame, since Keldor made it years ago, for a different house, where it fit perfectly. While he could have made a new one, we would have had to buy the material, and then it would have taken time to do the welding, so instead we just adapted this one to fit.

It took a fair bit of work to do the carving. The first iteration was done in only 1.5 hours, but then the door still didn't fit, as I had measured the bottom of the door, and cut away that much, but it turns out that it isn't quite right angles, so the bottom corner needs a bit extra room.

![[2026-11-14 carved.jpg]]

I carved away that bit, as Keldor was busy preparing the board that will go next to the door to fill that gap, I also did the sanding to remove the chisel marks. Sadly, after that was done and looking good we tried the door again, and realised that it is taller than we thought, and I should take it down another 1-2 mm Eventually, the space was big enough, and we intalled the door.

![[2026-11-14 gäller.jpg]]

Now I have put the old door on FB market place. I hope someone wants it, so we don't need to squeeze it into the attic with the other doors we have taken down.
