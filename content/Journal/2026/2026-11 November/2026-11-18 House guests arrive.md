Since I was heading to the office today, I brought the nice brown wool blanket that Torunn gave us. I think it looks nice on my office sofa

![[2026-11-18 office soffa.jpg|500]]

Then I worked till 11:30, packed my things and took a bus to the city center, where I got my glasses frame fixed. Then I took the next bus to the airport, arriving just before the plane landed.

Since it was snowy and slushy Sigrid, Browyn and I opted to take a cab to the regional bus station.  When we approached the line of taxis one driver asked if we had pre booked, and I replied "no, is it possible to get a ride anyway".

He said yes, and loaded our luggage into the car boot, and we hopped in. Just then a man arrived who had prebooked. The driver started to tell us that he couldn't take us after all, but the man said, no let them ride. And I replied we can squeeze in one more.

So he joined us, and we enjoyed pleasant conversation on the ride to town. The driver dropped him in the city center, and then took us to the bus station,  where they bought burgers, and I ate the lunch I had brought with me, but forgot to eat before it was time to go.

That took most of the time till time to catch the bus, which we took as far as Ånäset, where I left them with the luggage at the gas station and I walked towards the workshop to get the car.

Luckily, there was a car waiting its turn to get onto the highway, so I asked them fo a lift, and thus didn't have to walk 500 meters on the highway construction during busy traffic, on a day when they hadn't plowed away the snow berm, so the narrow road was even narrower.

After we got home Sigrid helped me with fitting the shoulders on my coat.
