Woke around 01:00 remaining the conversation from before we went to sleep, and decided to try writing about it, to see if I could figure out the triggers for my grumpiness on Friday. So I started by jotting down [[2026-11-22 road trip home|yesterday's]] summary, to give myself a little context. 

When we talked last night Keldor commented in reply to my saying that I have been been what feels to me unusually grumpy and snappy lately that the logical question is to look at what has changed lately to increase ny stress levels. 

When I woke today, the message from my subconscious was that my grumpiness recently is triggered when I feel what I read as "_unreasonable or unobtainable expectations_ from others or the situation. This doesn't make my snapping ok, but, perhaps, knowing that trigger will help me not snap in the future 

I felt that reaction from Keldor’s words on Friday, when he announced upon our waking from a nap, that we had 30 minutes to get out the door. I tried, but it was not possible to accomplish the last of my packing and loading everyone's stuff within that deadline.

However, my grumpiness when Keldor tried to ask me, as I was packing stuff into the car, about how we were going to arrange people into the cars, meant that I barely registered the conversation, replied that I didn't care who sat where, and promptly forgot that the intrusion to my packing had even happened, let alone what had been said. (This was a problem later, as things said later were not consistent with this reply, which he read as gaslighting, which wasn't my intent. Note to self, conversations go much better when one actually focuses on them, instead of trying to ignore them as a distraction.)

The other trigger for me this weekend was the feeling that I am failing meet any of Gilbert's expectations/wishes. He had let me know before the weekend that he really wants to spend time with me, and wants time alone with me, but life is busy, and it isn't happening, which makes me feel bad, and that contributed to being grumpy. I don't like feeling grumpy and snapping, but I am not certain how this is going to resolve.

My only meeting of the day was my last visit as part of the [SGNT](https://www.sgnt.se/) study, so they did all of the tests, including the one with the soothing blue lights and the electrodes on my eyes that always puts me to slerp, but, since I am supposed to have my eyes open, I have to keep forcing myself awake. 

At the end of the visit I met with a doctor to discuss the future follow ups to track my glaucoma.  They explained that normally they recommended to patients finishing the study to begin treatment of eye drops to reduce eye pressure. However, every visit I have had during the two years of the study, they have commented that my eye pressure is low. So, after discussinf it with the doc, we agreed that we will wait and see. I will return for an exam in three or four months, and if I have started to show problems with high eye pressure, we can treat it then.

During today's exam, they gave me drops to dilate my eyes twice, and drops to numb my eyes twice during the course of the tests and photographs. As a result, I didn't even try to work for the rest of the day, as I couldn't see properly, so I just caught the next bus home, napping on the bus. Good thing I had no other meetings on the calendar.

Once home I busied myself in the kitchen, making a lovely green soup from the spinach, zucchini, and broccoli I bought on the way home yesterday,  to which I added the leftover homemade noodles I had brought home from the event.

Keldor got home early enough to take a nap before it was time to go, so we got an hour sleep. Then we drove to Umeå for the Minnesfika (memorial gathering) for Ingemar. 

It was a nice gathering, and I made good progress on my coat, finally starting to work on the collar and front edge. I also removed the cuff, and prepared a better version I can take to work and sew tomorrow. 

We left so late we didn't get home till nearly midnight, so I did chair yoga as we drove, and then another session after we got homr, as my legs were aching, and that is the best way to make them behave.
