Having stayed up late last night, I slept in. Keldor didn't go to work, so after I settled into my computer station and started working he went to the attic intending to build the wall next to the plumbing ventilation that goes up and out the roof. However, when he got there he noticed that there was a leak coming down the outside of the pipe.

![[2026-11-13 a small drip.jpg|500]]

So instead he got out his work saftey harness the can of tar, and a metal spackel spreader, and we went out into the rain. He went up the ladder, anchored the rope to the chimney, adjusted to an appropriate length so if he slipped, he wouldn't go over the edge of the roof, and carefully worked his way over to the pipe. Once there, he carefully lifted away the clay tiles, applied the tar around the edge of the pipe, and then put the tiles back into place. 

![[2026-11-13 on the roof.jpg|500]]

I had the hard job. Wait on the ground and watch, so if he should fall and injure himself someone can call for an ambulance. He didn't. 

So we went in, cleaned tar off of everything and put it all away, tied a rag around the pipe, so we could see if it worked, and I resumed my work day.

Between my late start to the day, plus stopping to be there for fixing the leak, I wound up working later than planned, and didn't get in to town to see Gilbert. 

So instead I called him, and he kept me company as I spackeled the new wall in the other part of the closet  that Keldor installed while I was at the computer. 

Then I went downstairs, and Keldor and I rebuilt my computer work station. I am much happier with it now.
