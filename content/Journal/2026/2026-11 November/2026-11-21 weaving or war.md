Enjoyed the weaving and sprang workshops. Failed to get my test sprang piece started without mistakes. Tried many times.  When I kept getting lost in the crossings of my threads as I was paying too much attention to the next workshop, I tried moving my self to an empty place in the far side of the room to just focus on the project, as I really wanted to get the interlacing started completely correctly, to see if I could make it all work from a good foundation.

Alas, every time it started to feel like it was working, someone came by and asked how it was going, and as soon as I stopped chatting and looked at the project again, I got confused about what to do next and found another mistake.

Eventually, I gave up, returned the frame unworked, and resumed sewing on my coat 

Despite the part where I can't concentrate on sprang and talk at the same time, I really enjoyed seeing and chatting with people.
