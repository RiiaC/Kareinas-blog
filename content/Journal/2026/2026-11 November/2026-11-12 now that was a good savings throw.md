In the evening we experienced one of those moments where you know that I must have rolled a 20 on my savings throw.

Keldor and I were practicing with the weighted swords, aiming at the other person's swords, not one another. He started doing a weird looping path to his sword blows, such that I had no idea where my sword should be to block it.

As a result,  his sword hit mine way out on the tip, and so my sword hopped backwards enough centimetres to hit the upper edge of my glasses against my forehead just hard enough to get me to say "ow!", but not to any damage to me.

When I reached for my forehead to see if everything was ok, I discovered that the left arm of my glasses was no longer attached, but the lens was unbroken. Iit turned out to be possible for Keldor to just push the posts back into the holes in the lens, and now you can't tell it had ever "broken".
