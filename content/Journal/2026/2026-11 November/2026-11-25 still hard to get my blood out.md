Today I had a the follow-up visit with a doctor at the local health center that the physical therapist had suggested on [[2026-11-02 yup, that bit of hip is still numb|2026-11-02]] . The doc, Linda, asked many questions about things that could be symptoms of a real problem internally, but for most of them my answer was "nope". Seemed unconcerned about the numb spot on the hip, but did poking around on my tummy to see if anything can be felt. On the left side it was a bit more sensitive, and Linda wondered if that firm area is just a muscle or perhaps something that shouldn't be there, so she went and got a colleague, who also poked my tummy, and agreed that it felt a little different on the left than the right, so they decided to order some blood tests and an x-ray.

I warned the person who went to take my blood that everyone thinks it is hard to get my blood out. After several attempts, another person came in and tried, and on the second try managed to take what was needed for the tests. None of the attempts in either elbow worked, my veins simply roll to the side, but on the forearm, when holding the skin over the vein very taut, it was possible to get the needle in. Ironically, the successful poke was the only one that didn't hurt.

Due to needing to wait for the second doctor to be able to come in, plus the delays in the blood draw, it was nearly two hours that I was at the health center. Good thing I brought sewing.

I went home, had lunch, and then sat down to the computer at 12:45 to see emails with meeting invites for a meeting at 13:00. It turns out that Mattias had forgotten to put the meeting we discussed last week on the calendar till the last minute. So I asked to join remotely, and got even more sewing done.

After work Keldor beat me in a game of Qwirkle, then took a hot bath in hopes that it would help him get over his cold or whatever it is, and I updated my finances, replied to emails, and tidied up my recent blog posts so I can actually publish them. He went to bed a while ago, and it is closing in on 21:00, so I think I will do my yoga and head to bed myself.
