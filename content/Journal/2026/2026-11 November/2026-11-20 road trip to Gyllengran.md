Packed the chests for the event as Keldor drove to work. After the phone call I baked [[Gilbert's birthday cake]] and made a [[Red beet salad]] and cooked the other half of the zucchini with some eggs.

Then I gathered bedding and other stuff for the event. I had come as far as putting the mattresses in the car when Keldor got home and said he was going to take a half an hour nap. This soundedlike a good idea, so I lay down and woke an hour later. Oops, that left only a half an hour before when we had wanted to leave. It wasn't enough.

Gilbert had arrived as we slept, so I fed him birthday cake, and then resumed packing, loading the car, and getting things ready for the cats.

We finally started driving just before 14:00. Bronwyn driving my car, with me and Sigrid as passengers, and Gilbert and Keldor in Keldor's car.

We drove first to Umeå to pick up Gerdis, at which point I switched to the orher car, which turned out to be loud (but good) music. They gave me the back seat, so I could use my light that hangs around my neck to do sean embroidery.

I switched back to the van at the next stop.

On arrival to site I was surprised to discover that the event was divided between the main hall, and another building to sleep in that is a 10 minute walk away. (This info had been available, I hust hadn't read what was provided.)

I am not fond of having to sleep in another building when they are adjacent. I tried not to share my feelings about hving to sleep so far away, but i fear that I didn't achieve this goal.
