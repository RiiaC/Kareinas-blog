Despite getting home late from the *Minnesfika* last night, I woke at 05:00,  thinking of work. So, as Keldor slept in by my side, I used my phone to create a GitHub issue and record it in mt work Obsidian. 

Keldor woke at 07:00, around the time I finished what I had been doing for work. So we got up and got ready to go to work. Since I had my backpack packed by the time he was ready, I got a ride to the bus stop, arriving a bit early. We checked the bus app, and it had gone only as far as Yttervik by then, so we guessed Keldor would have time to drive about to where he leaves the highway to head towards Skelleftehamn before the bus arrived. 

Much to our surprise, he got all the way to work before the bus reached me, 10 minutes later than scheduled. When the bus started driving, I understood why it was late. Bad tires. It took five tries for it to be able to get back onto the highway. Each time the bus started forward, got no traction on the ice, and just sat in the middle of the intersection till oncoming traffic caused the driver to back up out of the intersection.  Each time the driver tried a slightly different approach, and we eventually made it on to the highway.  I had never before noticed before that there is a bit of a hill one goes up to turn out on to the highway, but boy did the bus tires not like that hill today.

My work day was busy, so I never took a nap,  but worked till the bus got me home at 16:00.

In contrast to my busy, productive day at work, my evening was slow and relaxed, in part because Keldor was feeling a bit sick, so he only wanted to sit on the sofa, so I sat with him, did some sewing, and some scrolling on my phone. Did my yoga reasonably early, and we were in bed before 21:00.
