During lunch I read the flyer and learned that Lövånger has two choirs. The church choir,  which performs in four parts during services, and another aimed at anyone who enjoys singing. So I wrote and asked about the second, and ut turns out their first practice of the term was today, and they invited me to come check it out. It was fun! 

I haven't sung in a choir since March of 2020! Around the time choirs were starting to meet again I moved to Lövånger.  I think I will go again next week.
