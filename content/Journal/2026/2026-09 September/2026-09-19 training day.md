Since we had a handful of guests already last night, we sat up late talking, which translated to my disinterest in getting up this morning, but I managed to drag myself out of bed just after 08:00.

I woke up thinking about my [[My new helmet and gorget#gorget 2026-09-16 a sad day for Lövånger 2029-09-16|gorget]] that needs dishing, and realised that I am a little hesitant to start that part of the project.
1. Yes, I made my [[Bacinet helmet]] and other armour, and the dishing went well, but that was more than 30 years ago.
2. I have never made a metal gorget, nor seen this style being made
3. These pieces are stainless steel, which I have heard is harder to dish than ordinary steel.
On the other hand, when I made the pattern, I did all the dishing of the cardboard without hesitation, so clearly my arms remember something about cold metal armouring.
