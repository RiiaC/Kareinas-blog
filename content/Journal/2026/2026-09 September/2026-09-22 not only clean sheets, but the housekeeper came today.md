Today I worked from home, which meant I was able to wash bedsheets. 

I also used my lunch to annotate a photo of [[Bryan's Coat]] pieces, and write up a table of parts and a sketch of how to assemble them.

After Keldor got home he planed a chunk of board to make a list to cover the metal rod in which the door to the attic loo slides. Then he sketched an eagle head on one end, and did a rough pencil sketch of a 4-strand interlace pattern. 

Since I care deeply and passionately about the over-under patter of knot work, he let me clean it up and ink it to be certain that all overs are followed by an under, and vice versa. The fact that the interlace disappears behind the frame further complicates this, but I got it to work.
