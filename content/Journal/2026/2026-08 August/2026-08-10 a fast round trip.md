The morning's plan was to go to my eye appointment, and then on to my office and work there for the forst time since June.

It turns out that I didn't come into the office today after all. I started walking towards uni from the hospital after my glocoma study appointment, and it turns out one can't get to our side of campus from there.

I tried going around the construction by continuing past it, and hit a dead end. At which point I checked my phone, and the next bus to Lövånger was in 9 minutes.  So instead of walking all the way around the other direction, I gave up and caught the bus home.

After a nap, to let my eyes recover from having been dilated, and cooking some food, and a handful of useful procrastination tasks, I finally settled in to work, and made good progress, and, despite my late start, managed seven hours for the day.

Then Keldor showed me what he had accomplished in the attic as I worked, starting to add the back wall to the final set of what will be built in closets, and we discussed what steps are next.

Then I spent an hour putting spackel on the wall that is ready for it, before doing my evening yoga.
