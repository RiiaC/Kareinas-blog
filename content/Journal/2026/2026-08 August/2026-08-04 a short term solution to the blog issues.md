Neither Keldor or I were feeling our best, so we slept in today. I finally got up at 08:00 as I should really take my vitamins for the [SGNT study](https://www.sgnt.se/), and the cats wanted breakfast.I sat at the computer a little and poked at my blogs. The creator of [Quartz Syncer](https://saberzero1.github.io/quartz-syncer-docs/) had replied to my request yesterday for help with my issues in making it work that the problem should go away with the release of version 2.0, which they are working on. So in the meantime I set it up so that I can just use Total Commander to synchronise my Obsidian Vault to the content folder of Quartz Syncer on my computer, and then use GitHub desktop to push it to the blog, and I am already much happier.

When I first set up Quartz, I misunderstood how it works, and thought I had to put Quartz into my Obsidian vault, and then move everything I originally had there to the sub-folder "content". This worked, but I didn't much like it, as I had all that extra stuff in the way. Now that the Quartz stuff is in its own folder again, I am much happier. I celebrated by doing a little clean up of the blog, moving folders around, renaming them, etc.

Then I went back to bed and slept several more hours, which I needed. This afternoon I have mostly taken it easy, but I did help be an extra pair of hands while Keldor hung up the new door in the downstairs bathroom:
