# an attempt at fixing my helm
After shopping we went to the workshop, where Kalle made progress on his armour, Gilbert worked on something,  Bryan relaxed, and Keldor and I looked at  [[My new helmet]] in progress.

As Keldor had put a lot of time into making the decorative grill on that helmet when he made it a couple of years ago, we wanted to see if we could re-use that bit for the modified version inspired by Helga's sports helm.

At first, it looked like if we cut it down to more of a triangle over the face it would work. Keldor did the initial cuts, and we tried tacking it back to the helm top, and put it on me, but it was clear thst it would need to be an even smaller triangle, so he marked the lines of what needs to go, and gave me the angle grinder to grind away the parts we don't need.

At first I was a bit scared, as I know angle grinders can be dangerous, and I haven't really used them before. But soon I was just enjoying it. It was fun to see the metal dissappear,  and mske the edges nice and smooth.

![[2026-08-22 angle grinder.jpeg|500]]

While I did that Keldor checked something on the car, and had coffee with Bryan (and ate the lunch he'd left in the fridge at work the other day, and then spent the rest of the week at home).

When I was done Keldor welded the other two attachment points of the grill to the helm top, but that change effected how the grill sits on the helmet, so now the point was in contact with the point of my chin.

This wouldn't be safe, so Keldor took over the angle grinder, and tried taking away even more, but soon it was clear that the curve of the grill is simply wrong for the new planned helm style, and there was nothing to be done about it. (I asked, onr can't just warm and dish a grill assembled of weled bits.)

So we sadly cut off the grill and put it into the scrap metal bin. We will try making a cardboard template before trying again.
