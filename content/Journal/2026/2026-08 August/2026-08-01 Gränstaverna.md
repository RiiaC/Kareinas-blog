Woke at 08:30 Finish time, but stayed in bed, updating yesterday's log for more than an hour before going off in search of breakfast. 

I enjoyed a leisurely breakfast, chatting with people, till it was time for my dance workshop at 11:00. We had six to eight dancers, most of them new to SCA dancing. We had a lot of fun, and danced a handful of dances, some of them multiple times.

Dance was scheduled till 13:00, so we stopped then. In theory lunch was to have been served a half an hour later, but that proved to be optimistic. The pie came out at ~14:20, and the pea soup at ~15:00. It turns out that frozen peas do not thaw overnight when there are so many packages of them in close proximity. This severely impacts the amount of time needed to cook them.

But the pies were not only yummy, they were also cute!
