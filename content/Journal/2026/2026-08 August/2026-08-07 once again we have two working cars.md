Then I exchanged another emsil with Carol James, who may be visiting in November, and if so, will do a sprang workshop. She had asked what we'd be interested in, and I said "tights!" She replied:

> If you’re serious about sprang leggings … then … while they can be made as single-layer cloth, I strongly suspect that double-layered cloth is a very good idea, have used the double-layered cloth technique for all my sprang leggings.
> 
> What I did is that I began at the ankle with single layered cloth. When I got to mid-thigh height, I added another layer of warp, a supplementary warp, and began creating two-layered cloth. The two-layered cloth has a pattern, the threads squeezing between to change layers, the cloth naturally sits wider … covering more real estate.
> 
> You might want to be familiar, actually very comfortable with, creating double-layered sprang cloth before trying leggings. 
> 
I’ve taught a class on double-layered interlinking. It’s now part of the sprang video library available on my website.

So I went to her page, subscribed to the video library,  and started watchingthrough them in order.
