In the evening I got distracted by finally making a note for [[My first bliaut]], and, in the process, trying to track down a better photo of Chartres Cathedral statue LP.LI than I had photocopied from a book in 2007. It turns out that book, [Sculptors of the west portals of Chartres Cathedral : their origins in Romanesque and their role in Chartrain sculpture : including The west portals of Saint-Denis and Chartres, Harvard, 1952](https://archive.org/details/sculptorsofwestp0000stod/mode/1up) is available online, and the statue whose neckline embroidery I copied for my bliaut is on the right side of figure nine on page 124 of the paper book, or 144/275 of the scanned version, which made it possible to find an [online version of the image](https://etc.usf.edu/clippix/pix/chartres-cathedral-ascension-portal-left-jamb-figures-and-decorated-colonnettes_large.jpg), which I have also saved a copy of (as yesterday the image wouldn't open, and thus I don't know if the link will break again tomorrow).
