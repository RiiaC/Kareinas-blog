[[2026-02-04 the new room is getting walls]] I spent a little time browsing the [Rune Database](https://runor.raa.se/) to see how certain words are used on rune stones, using the search function for a single letter in the relevant word, then using shift-F7 to check the results for the words I wanted (see below). Because those results are shown in several languages, including Old Norse, Swedish, and English, I could search for either English or Swedish spellings, and then see how the the word was spelled on the stone. Then, for some of them, we can cross-reference för the rune stone number on [Runkartan](https://runkartan.se/), where they have transcribed the runes themselves, using the [Futhark rune font.](https://runkartan.se/futhark-runes-font/download/) and see which runes were used. Once I did all that, I took a photo of the object into CorelDraw, added a curve where I wanted the runes to sit, then used the "fit text to curve" option to make the runes follow the path, and then sent him the photo of the result, to use as an example when he does the actual carving.  We went with this for the hammer Keldor made for Etienne.
