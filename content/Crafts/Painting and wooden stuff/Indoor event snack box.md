- On [[2026-09-27 Reengardas hantverkträff|2026-09-27]] I woke thinking of things we need to bring this weekend, and thought that we should finally get around to  buying and decorating yet another Jula box for bringing snacks to indoor events. Especially as Keldor has been smoking cheese and meat to share at events. So it would be smart to make a little foam lined ice chest to keep it in on site.
- So, on our way to the crafts afternoon we bought the box, at the meeting Keldor sketched a design for the lid, and that evening I painted the base coats of paint on the box (a pale grey "morning haze" on the outside, and some of the yellow wet room paint on the inside, as that will be easier to wash if anything in the ice chest ever leaks).
