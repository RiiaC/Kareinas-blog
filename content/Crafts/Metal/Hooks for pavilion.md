Many years ago I bought a bunch of second hand meat hooks for hanging stuff in the pavilion. I never liked the sharp points, and some of them were not open enough to hang properly from the poles.

So [[2026-07-25 Vallen Barnens Dag|2026-07-25]] at the smithy in Vallens Dag, we fixed them. 
- Keldor showed me how to do the first half of the first, I did the second half
- I started the second, Keldor took over and showed me another technique 
- Keldor did one completely himself 
- I did several on my own
# Tutorial photos
When there was only one left to do, it occurred to me that it could be nice to have a photo record of How-To, so he did the last one, and I took photos

## Step 3: draw out the end a bit
- [ ] strike perpendicular to the tongs holding the hot metal
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_13.jpg|200]]
- [ ] rotate 90°, and strike again
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_14.jpg|200]]
- [ ] repeat often enough to turn the end of the rod into a squarish profile that is longer and narrower than you started with
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_15.jpg|200]]
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_D.jpg|300]]
## Step 4: make a tight rolled curl at that end
- [ ] bend the point over the edge of the anvil
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_16.jpg|200]]
- [ ] rotate it and hit the bent part to curl it further
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_17.jpg|200]]
- [ ] keep turning and hitting at various angles till it rolls up nicely
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_18.jpg|200]]
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_19.jpg|200]]
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_20.jpg|200]]
![[2026-07-25_Vallen_E.jpg|300]]
