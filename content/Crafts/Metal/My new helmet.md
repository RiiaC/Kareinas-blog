# Changing my new helmet to work without a chin-cup
## 2026-05-31
we saw [[2026-05-31 a little basket#Helgas helm|Helga's helmet]], which is designed not to need a chin cup, because it has hinges that lets it close exactly around one's head. Helga cautions *"this is a sports helm; it is not authentic"*. but I liked the approach so much, we decided to try re-doing the helmet Keldor made for me to fit.
