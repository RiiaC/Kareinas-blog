First do one pass straight up-down-up-down etc. then do same technique as at Cudgel War--bring right three from back to the front, but exchange outer two and push what had been outer edge back over its neighbour and to the back again. Then alternate threads putting front behind and vice versa. when there are only three front left push the first behind and cross the next over the final and to the back.
