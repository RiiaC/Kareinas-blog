A month or so before I finished writing my thesis, Keldor told me that he wants a Viking style kaftan like one he’d seen a photo of on FB, and I made a mental note to make him one when I finished my thesis. Just as I finished my thesis Stigot and Lo decided to give Keldor a writ, so I have decided to combine this wish list item with a bit of wearable regalia.  I have some nice wool twill for the coat itself, and enough scraps of pretty coloured silk and wool fabric to do a rainbow wreath for the back of the coat, and Libby said she would do some tablet woven trim.
If anyone else would like to join in the fun, feel free to decorate a 10 cm circle of fabric in any manner you think he might enjoy (his favourite colour is “Färgeglad”, get them to me at your earliest convenience (but at least by the first weekend of Double Wars, so there is time to attach them to the coat), and I will add them to the coat in the positions of the mottled green circles of the rough-sketch coat idea. Please let me know if you want to do one, so I can keep track of how many are coming.  Around 10 total would nicely fit around the hem.
