# refurbished 2019
2019-12-08 I finally started working on the alterations this has been needing.  The goals are:
- make the coat a bit bigger by adding new, full length side and sleeve gores of the narrow black wool
![[2019-12-08 back side panel to triangle gore.jpg.jpg|500]]
- replace the pocket with a bigger pocket
- add an under-pocket slit so I can get to my keys in the phone baldric pocket without taking off the coat
- replace the shredded lining in the sleeves with brown linen
![[2012-12-08 trim sleeve edges.jpg|500]]
On that day I got it wearable enough to be able to wear it to work tomorrow, and the left side is mostly done.  The right side, however, has yet to be started.  ~7 hours to do that much. The right side should be faster, as the thinking parts are done (and notes taken), and the new side panel and sleeve lining has already been cut and is ready to attach.

The right side was done enough to wear as of 2020-02-18.
See https://trello.com/c/uepj15Wm for more details and checklist
