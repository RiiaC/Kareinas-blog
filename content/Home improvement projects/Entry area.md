The house came with two front doors. One that opens to outside, then a tiny room with a door to the left to the attic, and a door in front to the square central entry hall area. The tiny room by the front door was useful in the winter as extra cold space our of cats' reach for keeping food over night, but mostly itcwas just annoying, as both the outer door and the attic door opened into it,making it hard to carry stuff from outside to the attic without taking the attic door off the hinges.
# Door fix
In conjunction with the [[Create an attic bedroom]] project, we decided to get rid the door to the attic and the one to the entryway area, which would let heat circulate up the stairs to the bedroom. 

But we need a second front door to keep Kali from running out the second we open the door. So on [[2026-11-14 a busy Saturday|2026-11-14]] we installed the wrought iron door Keldor made years ago.
Step one was to chisel away a 1 cm deep bit of the threshold, as the door is a bit too tall for this door-frame, since Keldor made it years ago, for a different house, where it fit perfectly. While he could have made a new one, we would have had to buy the material, and then it would have taken time to do the welding, so instead we just adapted this one to fit.
