# second leak
- On [[2026-11-13 leak fixed|2026-11-13]] we discovered and fixed another leak. Keldor went to work on [[Create an attic bedroom]],  intending to build the wall next to the plumbing ventilation that goes up and out the roof. However, when he got there he noticed that there was a leak coming down the outside of the pipe.
![[2026-11-13 a small drip.jpg|200]]

So instead he got out his work saftey harness the can of tar, and a metal spackel spreader, and we went out into the rain. He went up the ladder, anchored the rope to the chimney, adjusted to an appropriate length so if he slipped, he wouldn't go over the edge of the roof, and carefully worked his way over to the pipe. Once there, he carefully lifted away the clay tiles, applied the tar around the edge of the pipe, and then put the tiles back into place.
![[2026-11-13 on the roof.jpg|200]]

I had the hard job. Wait on the ground and watch, so if he should fall and injure himself someone can call for an ambulance. He didn't. 

So we went in, cleaned tar off of everything and put it all away, tied a rag around the pipe, so we could later check to see if it worked (the rag was dry the next day)
