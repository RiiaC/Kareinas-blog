The Swedish word "*gryta*" means "pot", but it can also refer to a one-pot meal like a stew or pasta sauce. It can even be used for soup, though those are more often *soppa*". This note will record various ones I make that I both liked enough to record  and felt I had time to write.

# [[2026-01-17 game night]] vegetable tomato sauce
This is one Keldor and I made together, each tossing whatever they liked into the pot, and the result was yummy.I added:
**I added**:
- 1 box crushed tomato 
- 1 glass jar tomato paste
- 1 box chickpeas (and their water)
- mushroom powder 
- dried nettles
- frozen spinach
- green herbs (including mint, basil, rosemary, oragnao, thyme  marjoram, cilantro, tarragon)
- spices (including roasted gatlic pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, chilli, coriander powder, cumin, nutmeg, curry powder, cardamon,  wasabi powder)
**Keldor added:**
- 1 box lentils (and their water)
- 4 or five cloves coarse choped fresh garlic
- 1 fresh chill, chopped, most seeds discarded because he knows that I don't want the heat (if I weren't eating he would have added the seeds, and probably the other remaining fresh chilli)
- A handful of [Västerbottensost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4sterbotten_cheese)
- a handful of [finlly chopped ready to eat frozen kebab](https://www.jarvsokebab.se/)
- a quarter can of Guinness beer
