# almond yoghurt plain cake
Version invented [[2026-10-29 another slab in place|2026-10-29]]
- 0.33 c butter
- 0.5 c almond meal
- 2 eggs
- 1 t baking powder 
- 1 c flour
- 1 c yoghurt 
Cream butter with almond meal. Beat in the eggs, add the rest, beat well, bake in a buttered glass square cake pan.

I baked it till just solidified, but still white, for a yummy, soft, moist cake. So good I ate 1/4 batch still hot from the oven.
