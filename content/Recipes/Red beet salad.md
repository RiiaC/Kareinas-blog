# ingredients 
- 1 package pre-cooked red beets (~ 3 cups)
- 2 bags fresh spinach (~ 2 cups)
- ~ 3 cups chopped appke
- ~ 1 c almonds
# how I got this idea
In Sweden rödbetssallad is a thing, and one I don't eat as the creamy pink sauce includes vinegar.  So during Medeltidsdagar 2026, when they said that the vegetarian option for lunch one day was rödbetssallad, I was afraid. But the cook knew that I don't eat vinegar, so he made me one without sauce, and I loved it. Cooked beets, raw apples, salad, almonds. That's it. Yum! 

I have made it for myself many times since then, as one can buy pre-cooked beets in the vegetable section of the store.

# variations 
- use melon instead of apple. Not as good, but quite ok if a hose guest buys a melon that needs using up
