Made this yhe first time 2023-01-01, because my friend Raven in An Tir had posted her dad's recipe, and it sounded yummy. 8f course I had to adapt it, both for a smaller batch, and a liitle less sugar.
# Raven's dad's eggnog

half gallon heavy cream
half gallon milk
half pound powderd sugar
12 eggs
Nutmeg
Rum
# What I do instead

5 dl cream
5 dl milk
6 eggs (ours are smaller, so we rounded up to 4, and then added more)
45 g powdered sugar (dropped from 59)
(I don't bother with soices or booze)

Made it again 2023-11-28. My past self left no mixing notes, so I just tossed it in the big plastic serving pitcher and used the wisk of the immersion blender on it for a while, which put a froth on top, but when you pour the liquid comes out and the froth stays behind. 

On [[2026-06-21 the neighbour's garage sale|2026-06-21]] I tred whipping the cream seperatly, then whipping the eggs and sugar frothy before adding the milk, and then added the whipped cream.

# eggnog ice cream
When we make this, we usually we drink a a glass each as a beverage (Keldor adding whisky or ice cream to his),  and the rest goes into the ice cream maker.  It is really yummy!
