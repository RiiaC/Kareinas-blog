# 2023-03-19 Dandelion cookies, v3 large batch

1 c sugar 
620 g butter
2 c dandelion petals
5 eggs
Pinch salt 
2 t baking powder
1 1/3 c almond meal
8 c flour

Cream together the butter and sugar, beat in the eggs, add the petals (no green bits!), the salt, baking powder and almond meal. Add the flour a little at a time. Roll into balls of dough, 1 tablespoon each. Press with the shaker top of an empty spice jar to make a pretty flower-like imprint.
![[2026-06-04 dandelion cookies.jpg|500]]
Makes ~10 dozen
# 2024-01-25 half batch:
Did a half batch 2024-01-25, using 2 large eggs. Made the first cookie the normal way, scooping 1 level tablespoon of dough, but since the scale was already dirty, I weighed it: 20 g. So then I divided the dough into clumps near 220 g  each, rolled each clump to a log, cut it in half, then the halves in half, then each quarter into thirds, giving me a dozen cookies about  T each. Baked 55, and ate the rest.
