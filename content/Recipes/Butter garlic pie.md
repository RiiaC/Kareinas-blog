I tried a variation on two recipes I had seen recently. My friend Marion in An Tir had posted photos of a garlic pie she'd made, and it sounded good, so I wanted to try. That one was made ofom bouled garlic, then vaked in a shell with a white sauce.

The next day, I saw a recipe go past my FB feed for a Butter Pie, which also sounded good. That recipe called for boiling potatoes for 3 or 4 minutes  before slicing them thin and layering them with onions and butter.
I am not so fond of onion but I love garlic that has been boiled and/or roasted  therefore,  I had to combine both ideas in one pie. 
# First try, [[2026-11-15 a yummy Sunday|2026-11-15]] 
## crust
- 2 c flour
- 300 g butter
- 1 egg
- 0.5 t lemon juice
- 0 5 c water
- salt
## filling
- 900 g potatoes 
- 1 head of garlic 
- 150 g butter
- salt, pepper, nettle powder
Set a pot of water on the stove to boil and peel the garlic. Drop the garlic in the water and start washing the potatoes.  Add the potatoes to the water and set the timer four 4 minutes.

Put the flour, butter, and salt into a bowl and work them together 
(When the timer goes off, drain the potatoes and set them asside while finishing the crust)  Mix the egg, lemon, and water into the flour butter mixture. 

Roll out half the dough and line a pie plate. Thinly slice the potatoes, garlic, and butter, and layer them into the pie  i added salt, pepper, and nettle powder at about half way (and intended to add more on the top layer, but forgot)  roll out the rest of the dough, cover the pie crimp and seal the edges, ppoke a few stean holes, and bake till the crust looks just cooked. (This could have been a half an hour?)

Then I turned off the oven and left it to stay warm as we worked on other project. We ate it still warm, and it was really yummy
