While I mostly cook things that are quick to make, sometimes I buy a variety of fresh veg, steam up all of them, in batches, and make some sort of sauce from the steaming water. Some of these will get recorded here.
# spaghetti  squash etc in a spinach nut sauce
I made this one on [[2026-10-31 two days for the price of one|2026-10-31]].
I steamed a spaghetti squash, cut into thirds and seeds removed, over water with a bit of mushroom powder added. While it was cooking I chopped up som zucchini, broccoli, and carrot, and set them aside, ready to steam when the squash was done. Then, as the squash wasn't done yet, I made a [[Red beet salad]] and ate some of that. I also ate the pumpkin seeds, which I had toasted in the toaster oven as everyting else cooked. By then the squash was soft, so I set it aside and started steaming the broccoli stems and carrot. While they cooked, I mixed the squash with some butter and spices to get it broken up into its component "spaghetti strands", and then added the cooked broccoli stems and carrot to the bowl as I steamed the zucchini and broccoli flowers. When they were cooked, I added them to the bowl and put some spinach directly into the steaming water, covered it, and let it get soft. Then I used an immersion blender to make a nice green sauce, to which I added ground peanuts and almonds, some tahini, some ginger, lots of spices, a bit of soy sauce, some nutritional yeast, and more butter. I stirred that into the veg, and added some sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, and flax seeds. This whole process, including cleaning the kitchen afterwards took more than 1.5 hours, but the result was really yummy, and there is plenty to eat over the course of the week.
