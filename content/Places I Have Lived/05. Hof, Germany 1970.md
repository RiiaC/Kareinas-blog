# baby sister 
We were living in in Hof when my sister arrived. I remember dad telling me that I had a little sister named Beth, and asking "*Does Beth have any teeth?*", but my dad heard my pronunciation as "*Does Bef have any teef?*". It isn't surprising that I wanted to know  having often heard mom tell the story about the woman in her home town who had had her nipple bitten off by a nursing child with teeth, and that was why my mother hadn't been breast fed, having been born with two teeth,  one up and one down, her mother wasn't willing to risk it.
# grandma and nanna visit
I think we were still living in Hof when mom's mom, grandma  and dad's mom, Nana came to visit. They were a delight to have around, but they also used the opportunity to travel other places, while they were on this side of the ocean. Between their English, Grandma's Finnish, and Nanna's Polish, they were able to find people to communicate with most places they went.
