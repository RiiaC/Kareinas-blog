This is a place to share the various songs I have written or performed over the years for the various [Norrskensbard Competitions](https://frostheim.se/norrskensbard/).

Filling it in will be a work in progress that takes  while, there being more than 10 years of bardic contests I have entered. 
# 2015, Gerdis won
## There were four entrants:
- Astrid uti Erismarc (Frostheim)
- Gerdis av Bure (Uma)
- Kareina Talventytär (Frostheim)
- Þórólfr Blót-Úlfsson (Reengarda)
## My entries were:
1. Period Piece [[Prologue to the Canterbury Tales]]
2. Piece in a Period Style [[Winter time it has arrived]]
3. Bard's Choice [[Norra Nordmark]]
4. Three Words out of a Hat [[Genom merskog]]
Of which I got the most points for my Period Piece (presumably because I have more points in "recite poem" than in "sing", and the others were songs, because I love songs)
